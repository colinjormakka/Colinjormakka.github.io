How compound growth works

Compound growth means your savings earn returns on both your original deposits and previously earned interest over time.

  • Consistent contributions matter more than timing
  • Longer time horizons dramatically increase growth
  • Inflation reduces future purchasing power
  • Higher rates increase long-term compounding
