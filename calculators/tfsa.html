What is a TFSA?

The Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) is one of the most flexible registered accounts available to Canadians. Introduced in 2009, it allows you to invest, save, and withdraw money — all completely tax-free.

Key rules at a glance:

  • Open to all Canadian residents aged 18+ with a valid SIN
  • Annual contribution limits set by CRA (currently $7,000/year)
  • Unused room carries forward indefinitely — it never expires
  • Withdrawals are tax-free and add back to your room on January 1 of the following year
  • Investment growth (interest, dividends, capital gains) is completely tax-free
  • Does not affect eligibility for income-tested benefits (GIS, OAS clawback, etc.)
  • No age limit — you can contribute as long as you're a Canadian resident

Note: The age of majority to open a TFSA is 18 in AB, MB, ON, QC, PEI and 19 in BC, NB, NS, NL, NT, NU, YT — but room accumulates from age 18 regardless.
