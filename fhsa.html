What is an FHSA?

The First Home Savings Account (FHSA) is a registered account launched in 2023 that combines the best features of the RRSP and TFSA for first-time homebuyers.

Key rules:

  • Contributions are tax-deductible (like an RRSP) — reduces your taxable income
  • Investment growth inside the account is completely tax-free
  • Qualifying withdrawals for a home purchase are tax-free (like a TFSA)
  • Unused room from the prior year carries forward — one year only
  • Must be a first-time home buyer (no ownership in the current year or prior 4 calendar years)
  • Canadian resident, aged 18–71 with a valid SIN
  • Account must be closed by December 31 of the year you turn 71, or 15 years after opening
  • If you don't buy a home, you can transfer to an RRSP/RRIF — but the transfer is taxable

Note: You can hold multiple FHSAs but your total contributions across all accounts cannot exceed the limits above.
